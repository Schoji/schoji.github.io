Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

UntilDone does not collect, store, process, or share personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect user data, analytics data, or diagnostics data.

No Internet Connection Required

UntilDone works fully offline. The app does not connect to any server, does not use a backend or cloud service, and does not require an account or sign-in. It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your tasks, deadlines, and reminder settings are stored only in the local storage of your device. They never leave your phone, and we have no access to them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Notifications

UntilDone asks for permission to send notifications. All reminders are scheduled locally on your device by the operating system; no push notifications are sent from any server, and no notification content is transmitted anywhere.

User Data Logging

No user data is stored or logged by us.

Data Retention

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not retain personal information.

Third-Party Services

UntilDone does not use advertising SDKs and does not use analytics services that collect personal data.
